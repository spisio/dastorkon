Jasil means "green". It began in the Soviet years and you still buy it in glass bottles or by the cup from a fountain on the corner. Every factory and family has a slightly different herb mix, but tarragon is always the main one.

It is the cousin of the Georgian tarragon lemonade. Ours usually has a little mint as well.

<!--method-->

## Method

1. Simmer the sugar and water until the sugar dissolves. Cool for 5 minutes.
2. Blend the warm syrup with the tarragon and mint until completely smooth and bright green.
3. Strain through a fine sieve, pressing on the herbs. Stir in the lemon juice. Chill.
4. For each glass, pour 3–4 tbsp of syrup over ice and top with about 200 ml sparkling water. Stir and taste; add more syrup if you like it sweeter.
