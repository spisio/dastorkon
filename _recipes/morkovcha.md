The Koryo-saram were deported from the Soviet Far East to Central Asia in 1937, and thousands were unloaded in the Chu valley. Without napa cabbage for kimchi they made this out of carrots instead.

You will not find it in Korea. You will find it in every bazaar from Tashkent to Talas, sold by Korean grandmothers from enamel bowls.

<!--method-->

## Method

1. Toss the carrots with the salt and sugar and squeeze with your hands for a minute until they soften and give up some juice.
2. Add the vinegar and garlic. Pile the coriander and cayenne in a small heap on top.
3. Heat the oil until very hot (fry the onion in it until dark, then remove the onion if using).
4. Pour the hot oil straight onto the spices so they sizzle, then mix everything together.
5. Cover and refrigerate at least 4 hours, ideally overnight. It keeps a week.
